NODES:

INCIDENT
 - eventid
 - summary      = narrative summary of the incident (available with incidents occurring after 1997)
 - doubtterr    = 1: There is doubt as to whether the incident is an act of terrorism; 0: otherwise.
 - multiple     = several attacks are connected, and the various actions do not constitute a single incident (time or their locations are discontinuous) 1:yes, 0:no
 - success      = 1: The incident was successful. 0: The incident was not successful.
 - suicide      = 1: The incident was a suicide attack. 0:There is no indication that the incident was a suicide attack.
 - individual   = 1: The perpetrator(s) were identified by name (not known to be affiliated with a group or organization). 0: The perpetrator(s) were not identified as unaffiliated individuals.
 - compclaim    = 1: There are competing claims of responsibility for the attack. 0: There are not competing claims. -9: unknown
 - weapdetail   = pertinent information on the type of weapon(s) used in the incident
 - dbsource     = original data collection effort in which each event was recorded
 - addnotes     = additional relevant details about the attack
 - alternative   = identifies the most likely categorization of the incident other than terrorism.


DETAIL
 - nperps       = total number of terrorists participating in the incident
 - nperpcap     = number of perpetrators taken into custody
 - nkill        = number of total confirmed fatalities for the incident
 - nkillus      = number of U.S. citizens who died as a result of the incident
 - nkillter     = limited to only perpetrator fatalities
 - nwound       = number of confirmed non-fatal injuries to both perpetrators and victims.
 - nwoundus     = number of confirmed non-fatal injuries to U.S. citizens, both perpetrators and victims.
 - nwoundte     = number of perpetrators injured
 - ishostkid    = 1: The victims were taken hostage or kidnapped. 0: The victims were not taken hostage or kidnapped. -9: unknown
 - nhostkid     = total number of hostages or kidnapping victims
 - nhostkidus   = number of U.S. citizens that were taken hostage or kidnapped
 - nhours       = If "Attack Type" is "Hostage Taking (Kidnapping)," "Hostage Taking (Barricade Incident)", or a successful "Hijacking", then the duration of the incident is recorded
 - ndays        = same the nhours
 - divert       = list the country that hijackers diverted a vehicle to, or the country that the kidnap victims were moved to and held.
 - kidhijcountry = lists the country in which the incident was resolved or ended.
 - nreleased    = the number of hostages who survived the incident.


SOURCE (from 1 to 3)
 - scite       = source that was used to compile information on the specific incident


CRITERIA (from 1 to 3)
 - crit        = criteria


MOTIVE:
 - motive       = reports explicitly mention a specific motive for the attack


COUNTRY
 - country


REGION
 - region


PROVINCE_ADMINISTRATIVE_STATE
 - provstate


CITY
 - city
 - latitude
 - longitude
 - specificity   = 1: event occurred in city/village/town and lat/long is for that location
                   2: event occurred in city/village/town and no lat/long could be found. Coordinates are for centroid of smallest subnational administrative region identified;
                   3: event did not occur in city/village/town. Coordinates are for centroid of smallest subnational administrative region identified;
                   4: no 2nd order or smaller region could be identified. Coordinates are for center of 1st order administrative region;
                   5: no 1st order administrative region could be identified for the location of the attack, so latitude and longitude are unknown
 - vicinity      = 1: the incident occurred in the immediate vicinity of the city in question; 0: the incident in the city itself.
 - location      = additional information about the location of the incident


PROPERTY
 - property      = 1: the incident resulted in property damage. 0: the incident did not result in property damage. -9: unknown
 - propextent    = 1: catastrophic (likely ≥ $1 billion). 2: major (likely ≥ $1 million but < $1 billion). 3: minor (likely < $1 million)
 - propvalue     = the exact U.S. dollar amount (at the time of the incident) of total damages is listed.
 - propcomment   = non-monetary or imprecise measures of damage may be described in this field


WEAP: (from 1 to 4)
 - weaptype
 - weapsubtype


CLAIM (from 1 to 3)
 - claim         = 1: A group or person claimed responsibility for the attack. 0: No claim of responsibility was made.
 - claimmode     = modes used by claimants to claim responsibility and might be useful to verify authenticity, track trends in behavior, etc.


GROUP_NAME (from 1 to 3)
 - gname         = name of the group that carried out the attack
 - gsubname      = additional qualifiers or details about the name of the group that carried out the attack.
 - guncertain    = 1: The perpetrator attribution(s) for the incident are suspected. 0: The perpetrator attribution(s) for the incident are not suspected.


RANSOM
 - ransom        = 1: The incident involved a demand of monetary ransom. 0: The incident did not involve a demand of monetary ransom. -9: unknown
 - ransomamt     = the amount (in U.S. dollars)
 - ransomamtus   = if a ransom was demanded from U.S. sources, then the amount (in U.S. dollars)
 - ransompaid    = if a ransom amount was paid, then the amount (in U.S. dollars)
 - ransompaidus  = If a ransom amount was paid by U.S. sources, then this figure is listed in U.S. dollars
 - ransomnote    = any specific details relating to a ransom that are not captured in the other fields.


HOSTAGE_OUTCOME
 - hostkidoutcome = captures the eventual fate of hostages and kidnap victims.


TARGET (from 1 to 3)
 - corp          = name of the corporate entity or government agency that was targeted
 - target        = specific person, building, installation, etc.
